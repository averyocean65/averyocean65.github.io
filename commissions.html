Services

Songwriting

Songwriting is the complete package of services that I offer. Songwriting includes writing the composition for a song, and fully producing (recording, mixing, mastering) it with your supervision.

Mixing & Mastering

Mixing and Mastering is a smaller package, in which I will use the components of your project, and mix them together into a finalized song. I will also master a song that's already been mixed, though at the same rate at which I do both individual mixing and track mastering.

FAQ

Can you write lyrics and/or sing?

Short answer: no.
I don't write lyrics of any sort, you will have to provide those yourself, as well as vocals, if you want vocals on a song we're producing.

What is your process?

When working with commissioners, I like to keep communication as transparent as possible. This means sending pictures of the project and gear I use for the song, so the client can be assured that everything is 100% genuinely human made. I also try my best to send demos of the song as soon as possible whenever the client requests them. Alongside all of this, if the client wants, I am available to voice call and stream during the recording, writing, and mixing/mastering processes, so the client can have full control over the music and deliver constant feedback.

How do I pay you?

I mainly accept payment via Thomann gift cards (yes, I know, it sounds scummy as hell). This is, because traditional payment methods are either not available to me, or I don't want to share legal information about myself through vendors like Kofi or PayPal.
