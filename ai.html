Do I use generative AI in my works?

No. My work is entirely human-made and devoid of AI-generated content. I don't condone the AI usage in creative fields such as art and music, and I don't condone it being used to generate code. These are tasks people spend years mastering, some even get degrees for them, they're products of hard human labour, so why should I allow a robot to half-heartedly replicate that?
